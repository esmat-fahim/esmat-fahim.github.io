Kabul–Jalal Abad Highway — Urban Boulevard Adaptation

A corridor-scale adaptation study examining how the Kabul–Jalal Abad Highway could function as an urban boulevard within Kabul’s planned development structure.

Project focus

The highway was analysed as an urban corridor rather than only a through-route, responding to surrounding land uses, public transport, development pressure and the structure of the city.

01

Land-use analysis

Examined parcels and surrounding uses to understand the development pattern along the corridor.

02

Boulevard concept

Adapted the highway toward a more integrated urban-boulevard function within the city.

03

Public transport

Considered public transport and BRT relationships as part of the corridor structure.

04

3D communication

Used three-dimensional development illustrations and cross-sections to communicate the proposal.

Outcome

The study translated a major transport corridor into a spatial planning problem, linking mobility infrastructure, land-use transformation and urban design at corridor and parcel scales.
