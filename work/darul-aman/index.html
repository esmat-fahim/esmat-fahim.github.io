Urban Planning · Strategic Development

The Darul’aman Boulevard — Ala’odin Park Node

A strategic urban development concept for a major Kabul corridor, connecting public-land opportunities, transport, land use and detailed urban design around the Ala’odin Park node.

Project brief

The project examined the Darul’aman corridor as an opportunity for coordinated urban development. The work moved from a strategic reading of the corridor to a detailed development proposal around Ala’odin Park, considering accessibility, public transport, road hierarchy and future land uses.

01

Strategic structure

Identified development opportunities, major connections and the role of the corridor within the wider city.

02

Land-use planning

Developed detailed land-use proposals for the selected node and surrounding development parcels.

03

Transport

Integrated road development and public-transport relationships into the urban structure.

04

Urban design

Translated the strategic framework into a detailed spatial design for the selected node.

Outcome

The result was a spatially explicit development strategy linking corridor-scale planning with parcel- and node-level urban design. It demonstrates the GIS-based urban analysis and planning foundation of my later geospatial work.
