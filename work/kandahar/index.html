Strategic Planning · Urban Growth

Kandahar City’s Strategic Development Framework

A city-scale strategic planning framework coordinating urban growth, infrastructure, economic activity, corridors and neighbourhood integration for Kandahar.

Planning framework

The project considered Kandahar at regional and city scales. It identified important nodes and corridors, examined directions of urban expansion and proposed a more coordinated structure for future development.

01

Regional connections

Identified relationships between Kandahar and its wider regional network and development opportunities.

02

Growth directions

Mapped strategic directions for urban expansion and areas suitable for more compact development.

03

Development nodes

Identified citywide and regional nodes capable of concentrating infrastructure and economic activity.

04

Neighbourhood integration

Outlined ways existing and emerging neighbourhoods could connect to the broader city structure.

Outcome

The framework provided a spatial strategy for managing growth, connecting infrastructure, mobility, economic nodes and neighbourhood expansion rather than treating individual development projects in isolation.
