Housing · Urban Design

Incremental Housing for Eltefaat Township

A township planning and housing concept designed around incremental development for refugees and internally displaced people, combining neighbourhood structure with parcel-level housing design.

The approach

The project explored how a new residential township could accommodate households through a staged development process. A comprehensive spatial framework was developed first, followed by detailed parcel and street design.

01

Township structure

Organised residential areas, community facilities, movement networks and public spaces into a coherent plan.

02

Incremental housing

Developed housing concepts that could evolve as household needs and available resources changed.

03

Street hierarchy

Used pedestrian-oriented local streets and a clear movement structure to connect neighbourhoods.

04

3D design

Produced spatial and three-dimensional representations to communicate the proposed development.

Outcome

The final concept connected strategic township planning with a realistic incremental housing model, demonstrating how spatial planning can support phased settlement development and community-oriented neighbourhood design.
