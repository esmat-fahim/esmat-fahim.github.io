Informal Settlements · Community Planning

Resilient Community Pilot — Eid Gah Alif

An informal-neighbourhood upgrading concept for District 9 of Kabul, integrating infrastructure assessment, neighbourhood planning, public space and community facilities.

The challenge

The project focused on improving an existing informal neighbourhood rather than replacing it. Existing water, sewerage and electricity networks were assessed as a basis for an integrated neighbourhood plan.

01

Infrastructure

Assessed existing utility networks and identified infrastructure needs within the neighbourhood.

02

Neighbourhood structure

Developed an integrated spatial plan responding to existing settlement patterns and available land.

03

Public space

Introduced parks and shared spaces to strengthen the neighbourhood’s social and environmental structure.

04

Community facilities

Integrated community-oriented facilities into the upgrading concept rather than treating infrastructure separately.

Outcome

The project demonstrates an upgrading approach based on existing settlement conditions and infrastructure, using spatial planning to connect basic services, public space and community needs into one neighbourhood-scale proposal.
